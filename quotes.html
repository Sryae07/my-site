  8. Any fool can write code that a computer can understand. Good programmers write code that humans can understand. Martin Fowler
  9. Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away. Antoine de Saint-Exupéry
  10. A ship in harbour is safe, but that is not what ships are built for. John A. Shedd
  11. The details are not the details. They make the design. Charles Eames
  12. Talk is cheap. Show me the code. Linus Torvalds
  13. Design is not just what it looks like and feels like. Design is how it works. Steve Jobs
  14. If you cannot explain it simply, you do not understand it well enough. attributed to Albert Einstein
